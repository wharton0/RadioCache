RadioCache fetches audio while you are on Wi-Fi so it is ready when you are
not — then plays it back offline with full transport controls and
resume-from-position.

This app ships public-domain sources only and refuses to download
anything else. Two sources are bundled:

* Wikimedia Commons — per-file licence metadata there is mandatory, so each
  recording is verified redistributable before fetching.
* LibriVox — volunteer-read recordings of public-domain books.

No commercial broadcaster content is bundled, fetched, or redistributed.
The licence gate rejects NonCommercial and NoDerivatives material, and
treats an unstated licence as "do not download" rather than "probably
fine". Only public, documented HTTP interfaces are used; no access
control, DRM, or geo-restriction is circumvented.
